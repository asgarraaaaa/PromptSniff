Nonna's Lasagne

Serves 6. Takes about three hours, most of it waiting for the ragù.

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef and pork
  • 700 ml passata
  • 12 sheets of fresh egg pasta
  • Béchamel: 1 l milk, 80 g butter, 80 g flour, nutmeg
  • 150 g Parmigiano Reggiano

Method

Brown the meat, add the passata and simmer for two hours. Layer pasta, ragù, béchamel and cheese, five times. Bake at 180 °C for 40 minutes.
